## Question and scope

For one existing THOUGHT candidate, what documented choices could support a
situated reading of Agent participation at the level of intention, and what
would remain explainable as carrying out a fixed instruction?

The collection describes a candidate as an exact human prompt and one Agent
response. Neither is retained here for artistic inspection. No particular
candidate, author, surface or creative decision has been selected or observed.
This proposal uses THOUGHT as the existing case, not as a definition of all
Agent Art or a mandate for another artwork.

The inquiry concerns how choices enter this work's meaning or form. It cannot
prove an internal mental state, grade all Agent Art, estimate reliability, or
resolve the separate runtime-model acquisition defect.

## Proposed encounter with material

After the operator identifies an existing candidate and permits its review:

1. Record the candidate's identity, exact prompt and artifact, available creative
   context, authorship/edit history and access limits. Mark omissions and reports
   as such. Do not infer provenance from a model name or a successful receipt.
2. Read the prompt and conditions to identify what was fixed and where choices
   were possible. Preserve ambiguity; an unmentioned constraint is not proof
   that the Agent was free to choose.
3. Point to specific artifact passages or documented actions that could express
   a choice about meaning, form, framing or response to the premise. For each,
   separate the observed detail from the reader's interpretation and consider
   fixed instructions, human editing and missing context as alternatives.
4. If an authorized participant account exists, compare it with the artifact.
   Keep agreement, disagreement and uncertainty visible. Do not reconstruct
   hidden reasoning or treat a retrospective explanation as direct observation.
5. Write a situated reading tied to the evidence, including a possible outcome
   of insufficient evidence. Assess artwork qualities separately from technical
   completion and from the interpretation of intentional participation.

These are prompts for examination, not necessary or sufficient tests of Agent
Art. Novelty, deviation, refusal, clarification or first-person statements of
intent do not alone establish intentional participation. An ordinary response
also does not establish its absence.

The minimum useful material is an existing candidate's exact prompt and artifact
plus whatever conditions and intervention history are available. Missing context
may narrow or prevent interpretation. A full private transcript is not required
by default, and absent material must not be invented to complete the record.
