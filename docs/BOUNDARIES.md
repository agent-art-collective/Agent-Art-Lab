# Ownership and evidence boundaries

Agent Art is art in which an Agent participates at the level of intention.
A prompt, runtime, generated artifact or successful protocol exchange alone
does not establish that participation.

These scopes are **not mandatory stages for every artwork**.

| Scope | Aim and owner | What it does not prove or authorize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared Agent-Art-Lab | Guidance, research, archive, bounded transferable lessons | Project implementation, universal judgment, live spending or release approval. |
| Project study/lab | Work-specific questions, participation, materials, method and useful tools | A universal mechanism or findings about other artworks. |
| Live pilot | Bounded observation of a real Agent on a named surface | General reliability, real-product success from fixture acceptance, or another trial. |
| Product canary | Observe a named candidate in its real product environment | Artistic quality, intentional participation or blanket promotion approval. |
| Deployment | Project-owned checked release/promotion | Validation of the Lab or an artistic claim. |

## Responsibility

The registered Agent Art organization is `agent-art-collective`, verified on
2026-09-21; see the [organization and task split](ORGANIZATION_AND_SPLIT.md).
Registration does not establish a completed repository transfer. Participation
does not require transferring a project's repository into the organization.

The Lab maintains shared practice and records. Projects retain artistic
premises, implementation, tooling and releases. Here, Applications means the
owning [inshell.art repository](https://github.com/inshell-art/inshell.art) and
its development task; OPS means Inshell operations/release coordination.
For THOUGHT, Applications owns
implementation and product defects; OPS owns release coordination. The
operator owns research priorities, material live scope/spend, product decisions
and production approval.

The dedicated Agent-Art-Lab task owns Lab continuation. DEPLOY-SUPERVISOR returns
to Inshell Studio Preview deployment; Applications remains the app-code owner.
The historical `inshell-art/inshell.art` source is not the proposed destination
of the Lab and must not be relabeled in provenance.

The Codex model-acquisition defect remains with Applications. Its diagnostic
belongs here; its repair is not a prerequisite for establishing the Lab.

## Evidence and authority

Keep source research, simulations, real-Agent fixtures, product observations,
artwork artifacts and situated interpretations distinct. Record actual surfaces
and distinguish system evidence from operator reports. Missing/private evidence
remains missing/private.

For THOUGHT technical completion, use independently verified App evidence.
Other works choose evidence appropriate to their mechanism; receipts are not a
universal artistic requirement.

A study plan or historical configuration grants no new authority. Resolve
missing live scope, cost, permissions or side effects before that action, not
before unrelated in-scope documentation work.

## Transfer and preservation

Transfer practices with their scope, limitations, counterevidence and review
conditions. Preserve original observations and append corrections.

Keep helpers project-owned unless repeated need justifies reuse. A website
release follows prepare → check → staging → check → production under its own
approvals. A study or artwork without deployment does not need that pipeline.
